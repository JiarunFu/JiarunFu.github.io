### Research

I work on causal learning and counterfactual reasoning, uncertainty modeling, generalizable agent planning, trustworthy large-model reasoning, and multi-agent systems. My research follows a unified trajectory:

> **Mechanism identification → Uncertainty quantification → Interventional control → Complex-system validation**

- **Causal reasoning and counterfactual learning** for model and agent decisions
- **Causally controlled agent harnesses** for long-horizon execution
- **Uncertainty calibration and risk-sensitive decisions**
- **Distributional causal fairness** and responsible AI
- **Flow-based planning and cross-task generalization**
- **Causal multi-agent social simulation**
