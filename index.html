ACADEMIC PROFILE / 2026

Jiarun Fu.

Ph.D. Student · Beijing Institute of Technology

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Lizhong Ding. I study how intelligent systems can learn, reason, plan, and adapt beyond their training environments.

My research centers on statistical and causal learning, generalizable agent planning, and reinforcement learning.
