01 / ABOUT

Building intelligence that can explain why.

构建能够解释“为什么”的智能。

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Computer Science, Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Lizhong Ding, and a visiting student at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, collaborating with Prof. Xu Chen.

我是北京理工大学计算机学院博士生，导师为丁立中教授；同时在中国人民大学高瓴人工智能学院访学，合作导师为陈旭教授。

My research spans causal learning and counterfactual reasoning, uncertainty modeling, generalizable agent planning, trustworthy large-model reasoning, and multi-agent systems. I am particularly interested in turning causal evidence into operational decisions for long-horizon agents and complex social systems.

我的研究涵盖因果学习与反事实推理、不确定性建模、可泛化智能体规划、可信大模型推理及多智能体系统，尤其关注如何将因果证据转化为长程智能体与复杂社会系统中的可执行决策。

02 / RESEARCH

Six directions, one question:
how should AI act under uncertainty?

六个方向，一个核心问题：
人工智能应如何在不确定性下行动？

R1CORE

Causal Reasoning & Counterfactual Learning

因果推理、反事实学习与可解释智能

Identify mechanism-level factors behind reasoning and decisions through structural causal models, controlled interventions, and counterfactual contrasts.

围绕结构因果模型、可控干预和反事实对照，识别大模型与智能体推理、决策中真正影响结果的机制性因素。

SCMCounterfactualsAttribution
R2NEXT重点

Causally Controlled Agent Harness

因果控制的智能体 Harness 系统

Treat planning, retrieval, tools, verification, reflection, memory, replanning, and stopping as an intervenable orchestration layer.

将规划、检索、工具调用、验证、反思、记忆、重规划与停止机制视为可干预的执行编排层。

HarnessLong HorizonEfficiency
R3

Uncertainty, Calibration & Risk

不确定性量化、校准与风险敏感决策

Use multi-source uncertainty as a proactive signal for verification, fallback, replanning, and safety constraints—not merely a confidence report.

使不确定性成为触发验证、回退、重规划与安全约束的前置决策信号，而非仅用于事后置信度报告。

CalibrationRiskReliable AI
R4

Distributional Causal Fairness

分布式因果公平与责任人工智能

Move from mean-level disparities to counterfactual differences between predictive distributions, with auditable effect sizes and significance tests.

从均值差异扩展到预测分布的反事实差异，建立可审计、可比较且跨场景稳健的公平性检验范式。

FairnessDistribution ShiftTesting
R5

Generalizable Agent Planning

智能体规划方法与跨任务泛化

Model planning as continuous transport over meta-state distributions, shaped by value signals and generative flow matching.

将智能体规划表述为元状态分布的连续输运过程，以价值信号塑造目标分布，并通过生成式流匹配实现泛化。

Flow MatchingPlanningGeneralization
R6NEXT重点

Causal Multi-Agent Social Simulation

大模型多智能体因果社会模拟

Explain how interpretable micro-level interventions and network structure produce cooperation, trust, norms, polarization, efficiency, or collective risk.

通过重复博弈、反事实实验和机制发现，解释微观行动如何形成关系结构与宏观规范、极化、效率或集体风险。
