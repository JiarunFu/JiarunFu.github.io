Jiarun Fu 👋

Ph.D. Student · Beijing Institute of Technology

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Lizhong Ding. I received my master's degree from the University of Sydney.

My research focuses on statistical and causal learning, generalizable agent planning, and reinforcement learning. I am interested in building intelligent agents that can reason, adapt, and generalize in complex environments.

Google Scholar·DBLP·GitHub·Email

🔬 Research Interests

  • Statistical & Causal Learning — learning theory, causal inference, fairness, and trustworthy reasoning.
  • Generalizable Agent Planning — counterfactual planning, generative decision-making, and environmental generalization.
  • Reinforcement Learning & Agentic Systems — long-horizon decision-making, autonomous agent workflows, and feedback-driven adaptation.
